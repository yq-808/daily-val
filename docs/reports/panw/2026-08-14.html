3 · How we get the fair value

Each multiple gives one implied share price — equity multiples directly, EV-based multiples after adding net cash and dividing by shares. The fair value is the average of those prices.

4 · What the price is actually saying

Everything above was computed without looking at the market. Here we do the reverse — hold the same FY2027 figures fixed, take the traded price as given, and read off the multiple it implies.

5 · Reasoning to the price

A multiple on next year's figures is not the only way to defend a price — you can also be paying today for a later year. So: push the figures out to the company's own planning horizon, discount back, and solve for the one thing left over — the multiple the business would still have to command that far out.
